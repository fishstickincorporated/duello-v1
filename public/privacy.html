Duello Privacy Policy

Effective: November 3, 2026

Duello is a 1v1 word game. This policy explains what information Duello collects, how it is used, and how you can delete it. If you have questions, contact us at fishstickincorporated@gmail.com.

Information we collect

Account information you give us. When you sign up, you provide an email address, first and last name, and a username, and you choose a password. Passwords are stored only as salted hashes, never in plain text. If you upload a profile photo, we store that image.

Game information. We store the games you play, your scores, your Elo rating and Elo history, your game statistics, the words you play, and which bot opponent you played.

Sign-in and security information. We store session tokens so you stay signed in, and email verification codes and password-reset codes. Codes are stored hashed and they expire.

Technical information. Our hosting provider may process IP addresses and server logs to operate and secure the service. The app loads the Comfortaa font from Google Fonts, and Google may receive your IP address when that font loads.

What we do not collect. We do not use advertising trackers, we do not collect your location or your contacts, and we do not access your photos, microphone, or camera except for a profile image you choose to upload.

How we use your information

We use your information to run the game and your account, show leaderboards and statistics, send you sign-up and password-reset codes, keep accounts secure, prevent abuse, and improve the game.

Sharing

We share information only with the services needed to run Duello: Render, which hosts the app and its database, and Resend, which delivers sign-up and password-reset emails to your email address. We do not sell your information, we do not share it for advertising, and we do not currently use third-party analytics.

Retention and deletion

We keep your information while your account is active. You can delete your account at any time in the app, under Profile, then Edit profile, then Delete account. Deleting your account permanently deletes your account, your sessions, your games, your Elo history, and your verification and password-reset codes from the live database. Routine hosting backups, if present, are not used to recover deleted accounts.

Security

Duello is served over HTTPS, passwords are stored as salted hashes, and session tokens are random values. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, but we take reasonable steps to protect your information.

Children

Duello is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from children under 13. If you believe a child under 13 has provided information to us, contact us and we will delete it.

Your choices

You can edit your profile, change your password, and sign out in the app at any time. You can delete your account as described above.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted here with a new effective date.

Contact

Questions about this policy: fishstickincorporated@gmail.com.
